Your own content

You can change a card’s hook with “✎ Edit” or write your own, add your own words, add photos or have a picture drawn. Only you can see them: they are never shown to other users. They are stored on your phone and in your device-linked backup. You are responsible for what you add. Do not add:

  • illegal content,
  • someone else’s text, images or photos without permission,
  • another person’s private information, or a photo of them without their permission,
  • content that insults, threatens or harms anyone.

Your content stays yours. Only if you turn on Settings → “Contribute your hooks” do you allow us to use an anonymous copy of your hooks and stories, free of charge, to improve Word Hook’s hooks. You can turn this off at any time; what was sent before is anonymous and cannot be taken back.

Story, picture and pronunciation help

“Write my story” and “Draw my picture” are optional. The first time, we ask for your consent; you can turn it off in Settings → “Story and picture help”.

  • These stories and pictures are generated automatically by text and image generation models; no person writes or draws them.
  • For a story, the word, its meaning, your hook and (if any) your story are sent to Anthropic. For a picture, Anthropic writes a description of the scene and Cloudflare draws it. Both work on our behalf.
  • When you add your own word, only the word you typed is sent to Anthropic to find its pronunciation; it is not sent if you turned the help off or tapped “Not now” in the consent window.
  • A story or picture can come out wrong, odd or not what you wanted. If you see something inappropriate, tell us with “⚑ Report”; that result is hidden at once.
  • We try to filter unsuitable content out of the results, but we cannot guarantee we catch everything.
  • You may use the stories and pictures made for you for your own learning.
  • This help has daily allowances; the current numbers are shown in the app.

Backup and deletion

There are no accounts or logins. Your progress and your own content are kept on our server in a device-linked backup; they come back if you reinstall the app. If you set up a new phone from your old phone’s backup or by transferring from it, they come back there too. The backup of an app that hasn’t been opened for 24 months is deleted.

In Settings, “Reset progress” deletes your progress and “Delete my data” deletes the data on the phone and our backup. In both cases your own hooks, words and photos stay on the phone unless you tick “Also delete my own hooks, words and photos”. Details are in the Privacy policy.
